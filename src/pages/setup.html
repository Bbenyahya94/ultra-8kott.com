ULTRA 8K OTT
Device setup guides

From first click to first stream.

Use the Ultra 8K OTT guide that matches your device. The exact menus can change by player version, but the flow stays simple.

FIRESTICK

Downloader + preferred player

  1. Open Downloader and enter the installer address supplied by support.
  2. Install your chosen compatible player.
  3. Add your Ultra 8K OTT service details.
  4. Refresh the playlist and guide.
SMART TV

IBO Player or Smarters

  1. Install the chosen player from the TV store.
  2. Select Xtream Codes or M3U login.
  3. Enter credentials accurately.
  4. Wait for the playlist to load.
ANDROID & iOS

Mobile setup

  1. Install IPTV Smarters Pro or XCIPTV.
  2. Open the compatible login type.
  3. Add the details sent by your provider.
  4. Save, refresh and test an authorized stream.
ENIGMA2 / MAG

Receiver setup

  1. Open the plugin or player manager.
  2. Choose the compatible portal or playlist field.
  3. Enter the details supplied by support.
  4. Reload content and guide data.
Pick your player

The player app is your viewing dashboard.

Use a compatible player that you trust and that is officially available for your device. Popular names include TiviMate, IPTV Smarters Pro, XCIPTV and IBO Player.

  • Keep your player updated
  • Use stable Wi-Fi or wired Ethernet when possible
  • Double-check username, password and server fields
  • Ask for help before changing advanced settings
Ask setup support
Quick troubleshooting

Small checks that solve most setup questions.

Playlist not loading?

Review every login field, then confirm your device has a stable internet connection.

Playback buffering?

Try Ethernet, close unused apps and check the available speed on the specific device.

Need personal help?

Send your device model and player name to the support team through WhatsApp.
